# Visual Quality Rules

1. Mining must never trigger a graphics-resolution upgrade or downgrade. Interaction changes state, not quality.
2. Nearby pristine and damaged blocks share the same material language and micro-scale proportions.
3. Expensive decorative geometry may LOD by camera distance only.
4. Physical microvoxel occupancy remains authoritative at every visual tier.
5. Grass growth must remain attached to supporting physical turf cells.
6. Distant simplification should preserve silhouette, color family, material breakup and atmospheric continuity.
7. UI art direction uses the RuneForge forged-charcoal, bronze/gold and blue-gem language consistently across hub, HUD, inventory and future crafting screens.
